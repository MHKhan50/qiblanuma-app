# Review checklist for testers

Thank you for checking the app. Please note anything wrong with the screen, the surah and ayah, and a photo.

## Quran (most important)
- [ ] Open five surahs of your choice, including Al-Fatiha, Al-Baqarah (start and end), Yasin and the last juz. Compare the Arabic with your printed mushaf.
- [ ] Bismillah appears on its own line above each surah, except At-Tawbah, and is part of ayah 1 only in Al-Fatiha.
- [ ] Ayah numbers ﴿ ﴾ are in the right places.
- [ ] Search finds a word you know (for example رحمة).
- [ ] Long-press an ayah: it becomes a bookmark and appears in the Quran list.

## Qurani duain
- [ ] Each dua matches its reference (surah:ayah) in your mushaf.

## Prayer times (Hanafi)
- [ ] Compare today's times with your local masjid timetable. Write both down.
- [ ] Asr follows the Hanafi time.
- [ ] Reminders arrive at prayer time (allow notifications when asked).

## Qibla
- [ ] Stand where you know the Qibla (masjid or home prayer place). The mat points the same way.

## General
- [ ] Urdu and Roman Urdu wording is correct and respectful.
- [ ] Anything that looks wrong, confusing or slow.
