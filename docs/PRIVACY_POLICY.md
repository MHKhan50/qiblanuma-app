# Privacy policy

Qiblanuma, by Masmatix Innovations. Last updated: 27 October 2026.

Qiblanuma is free, has no adverts, no account and no tracking.

## What stays on your phone
- **Location.** Used only on your phone to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.
- **Settings, bookmarks, tasbeeh counts and khatam progress.** Stored only on your phone.
- **Prayer reminders.** Scheduled by your phone; nothing is sent to a server.

## What uses the internet
- **Quran recitation.** Audio is streamed from the Islamic Network audio service (cdn.islamic.network). Like any website, that service can see your internet address when it sends the audio.
- **Voice search.** When you use the microphone, your phone's own speech service (Google on Android, Apple on iPhone) turns your voice into text. On some phones this happens on the provider's servers, under their privacy policy. Qiblanuma does not record or keep your voice.

## What we do not do
- No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no selling or sharing of data.
- We do not collect personal information, so there is nothing for us to delete; uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.
